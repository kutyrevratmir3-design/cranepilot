# External real-camera benchmark preregistration, 2026-11-07

## Question and sealed data

Does the frozen CranePilot angular model transfer to a genuinely different crane, and how does it compare to explicit physically structured and standard recurrent/autoregressive references fitted under a common measurement contract? No new CranePilot architecture, no large search, no tuning on test. This is a new external prediction study, not hardware control, not a continuation of the 96-case simulation test.

Source: [Khemakhem et al., Behavioral Data-Driven Optimal Trajectory Generation for Rotary Cranes, arXiv 2605.14944](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14944), [author repository](https://github.com/ikhemakhem/BehavioralTrajGenCrane), pinned commit 1aa26b5c358e885325f75cc0e89ef613ff4b26f3 dated 2026-04-12. The repository README refers to an earlier 2024 version; manuscript arXiv v1 is dated 2026-05-14 and described as under review. No peer-reviewed status inferred. This source was newly discovered in this stage. No source data from this rig were in earlier CranePilot training/evaluations.

Use only RAW camera observations and prescribed profiles in the source's data/validation_data folder, NOT the original authors' 29 preprocessed training sequences. Author validation naming has no relation to our own partitions: IDs1–12 TRAIN,13–16 VALIDATION,17–20 TEST, fixed by numerical ID before seeing test values. One TRAIN sequence1, calibration, metadata and author scripts were inspected to qualify schema; no model predictions/fitting on it before this preregistration. Other raw sequences remain unparsed before fitting; test17–20 only parsed after selection/checkpoint hashes freeze. Downloader may copy/verify their bytes in advance without parsing values. All four test cases retained, no quality-based posthoc exclusions. Any nonfinite/nonmonotonic/missing/mismatched data causes a retained failed attempt, not silent exclusion.

Pinned MAT Git-blob hashes in config are verified before every load; a manifest adds SHA256 after download. Raw files stay in ignored local cache. Repository has no listed license; publish own code, metadata, checkpoints, numeric predictions and aggregate results, not copies of external raw files or author source. Reproduction downloads verified files from their original public host. Do not contact authors or post issues.

## Causal preprocessing and task

Fixed rope1m, boom elevation pi/2−.76rad and boom-tip height1.55m; camera intrinsics/extrinsics from author's fixed calibration. Reimplement the documented per-frame ray/geometry transformation; multiply angles by author's fixed scale1.7, which is source-defined, not fitted to our validation/test. No author MATLAB execution. Author whole-record mean subtraction and zero-phase filtfilt are deliberately NOT used for inputs OR targets. Source vision_data and synthetic/model yd from input MAT files are ignored. Initial camera alignment is retained; subtract one constant equal-record TRAIN mean tangential angle from all records. No per-test-record centering, smoothing, interpolation from future observations or fitted phase alignment.

Prediction target is tangential sway theta2 only; radial sway not represented by existing one-axis CranePilot. Input:9 native camera theta2/encoder boom-angle samples newest-first over nominal .4s;9 past prescribed velocity/acceleration samples;known30-step future velocity/acceleration profile. Future measured boom positions/sway/derived sensor velocity never supplied. Source raw first two numeric channels equal input profiles exactly (asserted); do not label them independent measured velocity/acceleration. Local tangential trolley-coordinate approximation x=R*boom_angle, R=height*tan(elevation), and acceleration R*planned_boom_acceleration. Angular coupling/centrifugal terms are omitted from the frozen 1D core, so this is a declared domain adapter, not a validated equivalence to trolley motion. The new rig's metadata omega=sqrt(g/1m) replaces the inappropriate Inteco geometry calibration for all physical/frozen predictions.

Task is index-based: origin k=8 onward, target k+1..k+30, prescribed command intervals k..k+29, nominal dt=.05s. TRAIN and validation/test stride2, complete windows only; windows may overlap but no record crosses partitions. Score horizons steps2/4/7/10/15/20/30 (=nominal.1/.2/.35/.5/.75/1/1.5s). Actual camera timestamps are irregular (schema record1 spans26.095s over499intervals rather than24.95s); timestamps are used for diagnostics, not as known future model inputs. Report actual horizon medians/ranges per record and do not call nominal prediction a strict wall-clock1.5s test. Input-to-camera physical actuation timing beyond supplied index alignment is not independently verified.

## Eight fixed methods, two training-access tracks

1. zero and persistence controls.
2. geometric: common causal forced-oscillator observer at native20Hz, omega from metadata, exact ZOH response to known planned acceleration. Assumes ideal tracking, a diagnostic reference.
3. fitted_oscillator: linear damped forced oscillator, globally fit omega,damping,input gain via multi-step output-error on TRAIN only. At each origin, profile unknown initial angle/rate from9 past angles and prescribed past acceleration. Parameter bounds and3 initialization points in config, best TRAIN loss, no validation choice among those starts. This is our grey-box PEM reference, not exact SMILE/LPV-paper replication.
4. arx_residual: regularized direct multi-output linear prediction of residual over geometric. Past theta/x/velocity features and command prefixes through each target only; no future measured states. Train feature means/scales, record-balanced ridge; alpha in[1e−4,.01,1] chosen by equal-record validation seven-horizon MAE. This fixed-length dataset cannot test length scheduling; do not rename it LPV.
5. lstm_residual: conventional encoder LSTM(3inputs,32hidden) over9 chronological samples, decoder LSTMCell5inputs(known velocity,acceleration,physical baseline,sin/cos nominal phase), scalar additive residual. Three seeds0/1/2, all ensembled;100epochs max,patience15,AdamW lr.001,wd.0001,batch128,clip2. Zero output initialization admits epoch−1 geometric fallback. Fit record-balanced MSE over seven horizons; checkpoint by equal-record validation MAE. No test early stopping. Not an exact Zhou PINN replication or novel architecture claim.
6. cranepilot_retained and cranepilot_candidate: frozen primary angular ensembles from the small research bundle, no gradient updates or validation selection between them. Native20Hz observations are expanded to41 nominal100Hz entries by STRICT previous-sample hold: at−10ms use−50ms, never interpolate with−0ms. Same .4s information, no invented sensor precision. Reference features zero because native Inteco xr semantics cannot be mapped to rotary commands; trained NeuralActuator bypassed. Predicted tangential acceleration is externally prescribed from the known profile, not future encoder values. State/context computed with the unchanged standard causal observer. Candidate still exploratory and not promoted.

Both frozen profiles receive the same training-only camera-centering constant and metadata conversion. They have no locally trained weights. The three fitted references have access to twelve new-rig records, so evaluate two DISTINCT claims: frozen transfer vs untrained physical references, and performance against locally fitted references. A locally trained reference beating a frozen model does not isolate architecture quality. A frozen-model win would still not prove full-native control transfer. All eight methods and all failures reported, no choose-one-test-winner policy.

Equal record weights (not pooled overlapping windows) then equal seven-horizon weights. Report MAE/RMSE,p95 absolute error by record×horizon; per-method cost/change/wins against geometric and fitted references only when relevant to prediction (no controller cost metrics here). Cluster uncertainty resamples the FOUR whole test records,2000 draws,seed7112027; use same draws for all comparisons. ## Literature status and next decision

[Zhou et al.,2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aei.2025.103269) describe PINN simulation pretraining and real-rig fine-tuning, but full architecture/loss/code have not been recovered; no invented exact reproduction. [CoDIT2026 LPV](https://doi.org/10.1109/CoDIT70676.2026.11631165) uses SMILE local-state alignment and joint optimization; abstract available, full details/code not confirmed. [Kusznir/Smoczek2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8749516/) motivates multi-step output-error comparisons and ARX; we do not reproduce their genetic search. [Kusznir et al.2024](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/14/8/3492) compares NARX-NN/TSF-ARX/MGGP and motivates including classical and recurrent references. The present common-contract implementations compare method FAMILIES; their numerical scores are not scores of those published papers.

No model search resumes automatically after test. Report whether independent-rig performance supports or weakens the current claim, and what experiment is necessary next. Synthetic control improvements, full hardware control, SOTA and publication acceptance remain separate claims.

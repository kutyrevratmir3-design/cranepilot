# Structural transfer: preregistration, 2026-11-07

Status: specified before development/evaluation. No hyperparameter search or selection in this stage. APHYNITY and historical runs remain unchanged.

## Question and connection to research

Does the previous causal lag/gain MPC benefit survive errors in actuator structure, a target reversal, and a matched-memory fixed-parameter observer? Rohrs, Valavani, Athans and Stein, *Robustness of continuous-time adaptive control algorithms in the presence of unmodeled dynamics*, IEEE TAC 30(9), 1985, DOI [10.1109/TAC.1985.1104070](https://doi.org/10.1109/TAC.1985.1104070), [NASA record](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19850063162), motivates testing unmodeled dynamics explicitly. This experiment is our proposed falsification test; it neither implements their adaptive law nor invokes their stability results.

## Frozen information and controllers

Same physical MPC, 6 command levels, 30 future 50ms steps, one committed step, ±0.8 command, ±0.2 trust region, 200 FISTA iterations, feature cost and 0/.25/.5/1 backtracking as adaptive_actuator. Calibration from the frozen primary research bundle; no new weights. Native xr zero; true acceleration-command plant bypasses the trained native actuator. All methods receive identical causal .4s theta/x sensor history and have access to 2s position/OWN ISSUED command memory at 50ms. No latent motor acceleration, true velocity, hidden delayed input, true gain, delay, friction, forcing law or frequency enters a controller. No probing/excitation bonus. The target changes only when the current time reaches 4s; controllers do not know it before that tick.

Nine methods, all reported: nominal; force_actuator (6 static scenarios); fixed_observer; fixed_force_observer; adaptive_params; adaptive_force; adaptive_observer; adaptive_force_observer; frozen_adaptive_force_observer. The last method uses EXACT previous observer qualification (ever accepted informative parameter fit AND current state-fit RMSE≤.001m). The two fixed observers and two new adaptive observers instead share qualification: ≥21 positions and current state-fit RMSE≤.001m, independent of gain excitation. This changes a gate, so it is a new ablation, not a retroactive change to the previous winner. No excitation is needed to estimate constant velocity from sufficiently long positions. All parameter-update grids, clipping, ridge, EMA, timing and observer limits are inherited verbatim. State fit estimates endpoint velocity/acceleration with nominal or adapted parameters; measured x and the common angular observer are unchanged.

## Hidden plant and test grid

6 latent states [x,v,theta,theta_dot,a_motor,z]. Raw RK4 100Hz with two .005s substeps, audit with four .0025s substeps. All angular dynamics nonlinear, same paired sensor quantization/noise and angular disturbance. Observable net acceleration is a_motor minus active friction, used only for posthoc metrics. There are no rail contacts/state clipping.

Six families: matched first order; command delay only; cascade of two lags only; friction only; gain change only; combined. Delays are integer raw samples (.04/.09s), hidden from the controller; command queue is initially zero. In a cascade z_dot=(K*deadzone(c)-z)/tau1 and a_dot=(z-a)/tau2. Friction subtracts fc*tanh(v/.015)+.08*v from net acceleration (motor state remains unaltered). Gain changes at 3.5s by factor .7/1.3; held constant on each raw integration interval. The controller continues to fit one first-order lag with gain, including on mismatched plants. Its fitted parameters are effective approximations, not identifiable true parameters on these families.

Exact four archetypes are in configs/structural_transfer_20261107.json, fixed before execution: L .28/.98m, frequency ratio .91/1.09, tau1 .075/.17s, tau2 .04/.085s, gain .86/1.14, deadzone 0/.02, angle 2.5/5.5deg, damping .02/.08, fc .025/.055. Nuisances are aliased intentionally in this small stress grid: no separate causal interpretation of their marginal effects. Same archetype across families permits paired structural comparisons. Each family×archetype has 4 exact/noisy×pulse/noPulse variants: 24 contexts, 96 evaluation cases/method. Pulse .13rad/s² on [5.25,5.6)s. Noise seed 1407112026; independent of the earlier grid. Development is 6 matched cases with L .35/.60/.85m×angle2/6deg, used for execution checks only. Freeze development hashes before eval. No tuning after either run.

Initial x .1m, target .3m at t=0 then .15m at t=4s. Duration 8s plus physical .4s warmup with zero input. Nine×(6+96)=918 episodes, 146880 decisions. Commands in observer windows end at the current tick; the committed next interval and hidden delayed input are excluded. RNG and disturbance indexed by physical time, common across controllers. Every raw state, measured sensor, issued command, chosen plan, forecast, fitted parameter and observer gate is retained.

## Metrics and preregistered gates

Tracking ISE and realized cost use the instantaneous step target at each control-grid sample; terminal target .15m. Trapezoid integration uses this declared convention at the discontinuity. Realized cost is the previous integral plus terminal penalties, without slew penalty. Sway ISE, tail RMS, peak angle, final position error, actual net acceleration effort, command variation, solver failures, current v/a RMSE and raw 100Hz rail/speed/angle violations are reported. Settling means remaining within final-target .01m, .02m/s and 1deg from a time at or after 4s through 8s. No interpretation of estimated tau as ground truth on cascade/delay/friction cases; report its tail value, not a parameter error.

Global gate versus nominal: strictly lower mean realized cost, sway ISE and tracking ISE; final position error≤1.01 reference; no more raw violations of each type; paired realized-cost wins>48/96; no nonfinite solves. Structural-transfer gate additionally requires mean cost AND tracking≤1.10 reference in EACH of six families, and no raw violation increase in any family. All gates descriptive on this fixed synthetic grid. Report method×family tables, matched fixed/adaptive observer contrasts, and cost-change uncertainty via paired 24-context cluster bootstrap (2000 draws, seed 7112026). No posthoc winner promotion to the forecast bundle or native/hardware controller.
